# CV and letter customs by country

Use the custom of the country **where the job is**. When in doubt: one page for entry-level and side jobs,
two pages at most for experienced people, newest first, no invented facts.

| Country | CV | Photo | Date of birth / nationality | Letter | Attachments | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Italy (it) | "Curriculum Vitae", 1–2 pages | common (`--photo yes` if there is one) | often (`--birth-date --nationality`) | "Lettera di presentazione", formal ("Gentile …", "Cordiali saluti") | rarely | The GDPR consent line is added automatically. |
| Germany, Austria, Switzerland (de) | "Lebenslauf", tabular, 1–2 pages | still common | often | "Anschreiben", 1 page, formal ("Sehr geehrte Frau …", "Mit freundlichen Grüßen") | **yes**: references (Arbeitszeugnisse), degrees, certificates in one PDF | Start date and salary expectation are often asked in the letter. |
| France (fr) | "CV", 1 page | optional | no | "Lettre de motivation", formal ("Madame, Monsieur,", "Je vous prie d'agréer…") | no | |
| Spain (es) | "Currículum", 1–2 pages | common | sometimes | "Carta de presentación" | no | |
| Netherlands (nl) | "Cv", 1–2 pages | optional | no | short "Motivatiebrief" | no | Direct, short style. |
| Poland (pl) | "CV", 1–2 pages | common | sometimes | "List motywacyjny" | no | The RODO consent line is added automatically. |
| UK, Ireland (en) | "CV", 2 pages max | **no** | **no** (never age, photo, marital status) | short cover letter or none | no | Achievements with numbers only if in the CV. |
| USA, Canada (en) | "Résumé", 1 page | **no** | **no** | short or none | no | No personal data beyond contact. |

Part-time, seasonal and mini-jobs: keep it short and practical. Put availability (days, hours, start date), languages,
licence and the most relevant hands-on experience first; one line per older job is enough.
