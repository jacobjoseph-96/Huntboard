# How each interview step changes the preparation

Always make `00_START_HERE.md`, `02_ANSWERS.md` and `05_DAY_SHEET.md`. The table says what else to make and where the
weight goes. Effort follows the time left: < 48 h → also `06_CRASH_PLAN.md` (what to do tonight / tomorrow morning,
what to skip on purpose).

| Stage | Who / what | Weight | Extra files |
|---|---|---|---|
| `recruiter_call` | Agency or in-house recruiter, 10–20 min | 60-second pitch; why you are looking; salary range, start date, location / remote, permit; questions to ask: who is the client, the team, the process | `01_COMPANY_BRIEF` light (agency + client if known) |
| `hr_screen` | HR, 20–30 min, fit and logistics | pitch, why this company, motivation, gaps said once with a bridge, **hard facts decided in advance** (salary with market range, notice period, start, relocation, language, permit); technical only as backup | `01_COMPANY_BRIEF`, `03_HARD_FACTS` |
| `async_video` | Recorded answers, often timed | short spoken answers (60–90 s), pitch, 6–8 likely questions rehearsed on camera; read the rules in the invitation (e.g. no outside help) | – |
| `hiring_manager` | Future boss / team lead | how you would do the core task of the job (a short plan in words), 4–6 STAR stories linked to the ad, questions about team and expectations | `01_COMPANY_BRIEF`, `03_ROLE_KNOWLEDGE` |
| `technical` | Skills test in conversation | the ad's requirements counted by area → priorities; deep `03_ROLE_KNOWLEDGE` per area; `04_RAPID_RECALL` cards; a mock with follow-up questions; if the interviewer is known, adjust to their background | `03_ROLE_KNOWLEDGE`, `04_RAPID_RECALL`, `07_INTERVIEWER` (if names) |
| `assignment` | Take-home task, test, case study, work sample | a plan with milestones, how to check the result, what to hand in (README / summary), how to present and defend every choice; the follow-up mail after sending it | `03_ASSIGNMENT_PLAN` |
| `trial_shift` | Shops, bars, hotels, kitchens, trades: a paid or unpaid trial | what to bring and wear, arrival time, the tasks of the day, the vocabulary of the workplace, hygiene / safety rules, how to show reliability and speed, questions about hours and pay; check that an unpaid trial is legal and short | `03_ON_THE_JOB` |
| `panel` | Several people (manager, peers, HR) | one section per person: what they will probe; raise the main gap yourself before they ask; answers for each viewpoint | `07_PANEL` |
| `final_onsite` | Last round, often longer / on site | the previous rounds' real questions answered better (from the debriefs); stamina plan; logistics (route, time, documents to bring); closing questions; references ready | `08_PREVIOUS_ROUNDS` |
| `offer` | Offer or negotiation | market salary range with sources, the user's floor and priorities, scripts for the call, what else can be negotiated (start date, hours, remote days, training), when to say yes | `09_NEGOTIATION` (instead of the standard pack) |

If the interview is in a language that is not the user's first language, also make `10_PHRASEBOOK.md`: memorised
opening, self-introduction, the key answers, vocabulary of the job, rescue phrases ("Could you repeat that?").

## The standard files
- **00_START_HERE**: interview details (date, time, time zone, length, format, link/place, interviewers, the job, the
  documents that were sent); "the few things that decide this interview"; what the job weighs, counted from the ad
  (area → number of mentions → the user's status → priority); a day-by-day plan where every block ends "said out
  loud"; what to cut first if time runs short; fill-ins only the user can answer (salary floor, reasons for
  leaving…) marked ⚠️; 20–30 mock questions.
- **01_COMPANY_BRIEF**: the company in 60 seconds, products / customers, size, locations, recent news, people (the
  interviewers' public profile if named), culture, an honest fit / gap table, sources (links).
- **02_ANSWERS**: the pitch (60 s), a table "every line of the CV that was sent → what they may ask → what you say →
  what never to say", why this company, 4–6 STAR stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result; 90 s each) taken from the
  story bank and the CV, gaps with a bridge, logistics, 6–8 questions to ask (pick 3), the closing line, a thank-you
  note template.
- **03_…**: the stage-specific file from the table.
- **04_RAPID_RECALL**: question → short answer cards, in blocks by area, for spoken drills.
- **05_DAY_SHEET**: one printable page for the day: timeline, opening sentence, 3 facts to mention, your questions,
  numbers you may need, recovery lines when stuck, a "never" list, what to do right after.
